About Us

Who We Are

ShoeLink is a professional footwear manufacturer and exporter, serving clients across Middle East, Russia, Central Asia, Africa, and Americas.

We specialize in producing and exporting a wide range of footwear including sports shoes, casual shoes, sandals, boots, kids' shoes, and safety work shoes. Our goal is to provide high-quality products at competitive prices with reliable delivery.

Although we are a manufacturer, we have established strong partnerships with 10+ factories, giving us the production capacity to handle large orders while maintaining consistent quality.
